# 🐍 Snake: Phase Merge

A **modern twist** on the classic Snake game—where **two snakes become one**, time is your ally, and strategy meets reflexes!

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Dual-Snake Gameplay**: Control a primary snake while a secondary snake mirrors your movements in reverse!
- **Phase Merge Mechanic**: Every 5 seconds, your snakes **merge into a single, powerful entity** that:
  - Grows longer instantly
  - Earns **double points** for food
  - Glows with a golden aura!
- **Wrap-Around Grid**: No walls—your snake teleports from edge to edge.
- **Dynamic HUD**: Track your **score**, **total path length**, and **time until next merge**.
- **Responsive Controls**: Arrow keys only—smooth and intuitive.
- **Game Over Screen**: See your final stats and restart instantly with **R** or the button.
- **Stylish Visuals**: Dark cosmic theme with neon accents and subtle grid lines.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Use the **arrow keys** (`↑`, `↓`, `←`, `→`) to steer your **primary snake** (blue head).
2. Your **secondary snake** (pink head) moves in the **opposite direction** automatically.
3. **Collect food** (green) to grow and earn points:
   - **10 pts** per food in normal mode
   - **20 pts** per food during **Merge Phase**
4. **Avoid colliding** with your own primary snake body—this ends the game!
5. Every **5 seconds**, your snakes **merge** into one golden snake for **3 seconds**—maximize your score during this window!
6. After merging ends, both snakes **reset to base positions** but retain your score and path progress.

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Plan your path before the merge! A well-timed merge over food clusters = big points!
